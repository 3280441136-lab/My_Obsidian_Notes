026 1.4通信系统性能指标​
027 Example 1:  In a digital communication system, the symbol duration is T=0.2 ms. The system adopts octal transmission (each symbol carries 3 bits of information).
028 ①Calculate the symbol rate R_{B} (Baud).
029 ②Calculate the information bit rate R_{b} (bit/s).
030 Solution
031 Symbol rate:
032 R_{B}=\frac{1}{T}=\frac{1}{0.2×10^{−3}}=5000 Baud
033 2  For octal M=8,log_{2}8=3
034 R_{b}=R_{B}⋅log_{2}M=5000×3=15000 b/s
