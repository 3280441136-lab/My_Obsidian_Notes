013 Example 2: Let X_{1} and X_{2} be statistically independent Gaussiandistributed random variables, each with zero mean and variance σ^{2}. Define the Gaussian process
014 X(t)=X_{1}cos(ω_{0}t)+X_{2}sin(ω_{0}t)
015 Find the mean value μ_{X}(t) and auto-correlation function R_{X}(t_{1},t_{2}).
016 Given Conditions:
017 E[X_{1}]=0, E[X_{2}]=0
018 Variance: D(X_{1})=σ^{2}, D(X_{2})=σ^{2}
019 X_{1} and X_{2} are independent ⇒E[X_{1}X_{2}]=E[X_{1}]E[X_{2}]=0
020 E[X_{1}^{2}]=D(X_{1})+(E[X_{1}])^{2}=σ^{2}, E[X_{2}^{2}]=σ^{2}
