Higher-order modulation gets closer to channel capacity.
Higher-order modulation brings higher BER, and good channel coding is needed to improve the reliability of the communication system.
高阶调制误码率更高，需要优良的信道编码来提升通信系统的可靠性。





--- PAGE 31 ---

Modulation: improves communication effectiveness (faster transmission, higher spectral efficiency) 
Channel coding: guarantees communication reliability (accurate transmission, lower bit error rate)
调制：提升通信有效性（传得快，频谱效率） 
信道编码：保障通信可靠性（传得准，降低误码）

Extension 拓展：MIMO communication
MIMO multiplexing复用: multiple antennas send different information to multiply data rate.多天线传输相互独立的信息，信息传输速率可以成倍提升。
 MIMO diversity 分集: multiple antennas send the same information to enhance system reliability.多天线发送相同信息，增强系统可靠性。
