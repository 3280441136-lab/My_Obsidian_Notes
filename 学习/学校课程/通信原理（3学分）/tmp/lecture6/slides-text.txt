===== Slide 8 =====
Summary
Wired Channel（有线信道）① Twisted-pair cable：Low cost, used for short-range LAN. Vulnerable to electromagnetic crosstalk.   成本低，短距离局域网（网线），易受干扰。② Coaxial cable：Better anti-interference performance than twisted pair; used in old CATV and surveillance systems.   抗干扰优于双绞线，早期有线电视、监控。③ Optical fiber：Excellent anti-interference, huge bandwidth, long transmission distance. Used for backbone & long-haul communication.   抗干扰极强、带宽大、远距离，骨干网、长途通信。
===== Slide 9 =====
Summary
Wireless Channel（无线信道）① Ground wave：Propagates along the Earth’s surface. Suitable for long-distance medium & long wave; capable of diffracting around surface obstacles. 沿地球表面传播，适合长距离、中长波，可绕过地表障碍物。② Sky wave：Reflected by the ionosphere. Uses short waves for long-distance, intercontinental communication. 利用电离层反射传播，短波，可实现远距离、跨大洲通信。③ Line-of-Sight (LOS) propagation：Microwave travels in straight line with no obstruction between two points. Applications: base stations, satellites, wireless bridges. 视线传播：微波，两点之间无遮挡，直线传输（基站、卫星、无线网桥）。
