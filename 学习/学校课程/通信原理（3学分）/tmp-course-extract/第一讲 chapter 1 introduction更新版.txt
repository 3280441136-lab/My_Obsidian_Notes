\n\n===== PAGE 24 =====\nPrinciple of Communications  Chapter 1 Introduction part 1 
重要术语解释
• Modulation 调制
释义：将信息信号加载到载波信号上，便于在信道传输。
• Demodulation 解调
释义：从携带信息的载波信号中恢复出原始信息信号，为调制的逆过程。
• 极简口诀：
调制 → 信息搭载载波 正
解调 → 从载波取出信息 逆
• 英文简短定义
Modulation: The process of embedding information onto a carrier signal for transmission.
Demodulation: The reverse process, recovering information from a modulated carrier signal.

● 数字通信系统性能指标的提取
The Beidou-3 navigation system broadcasts open-service and authorized-
service navigation signals on B1, B2 and B3 frequency bands.
B1 band (B1C signal)：Frequency range 1559.052 MHz ~ 1591.788 
MHz, bandwidth 32.736 MHz. The navigation message rate is 100 b/s, 
adopting QMBOC modulation.
B2 band (B2b signal)：Frequency range 1166.22 MHz ~ 1217.37 MHz, 
bandwidth 51.15 MHz.
B3 band (B3I signal)：Frequency range 1250.618 MHz ~ 1286.423 
MHz, bandwidth 35.805 MHz.
These multi-frequency navigation signals support high-precision 
positioning, velocity measurement and timing services. The parameters 
can be used to analyze spectrum allocation and transmission 
characteristics of satellite navigation links.
