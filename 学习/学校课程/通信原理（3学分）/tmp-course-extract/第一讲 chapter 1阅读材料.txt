Part I  Timeline: Major Breakthroughs in Telecommunication History\n(Extended Version — Including Ancient Pre-Electric Communication)\n≈3300 BC Sumerians invent cuneiform, the earliest mature writing system; written long-distance information transmission emerges. \n公元前约 3300 年：苏美尔人创造楔形文字，最早成熟文字体系诞生，出现远距离文字信息传递。\n≈3000 BC Ancient Egypt develops hieroglyphic writing for official record and message delivery. \n公元前约 3000 年：古埃及形成象形文字，用于官方记录与信息传递。\n≈1200 BC Beacon fire & smoke signal systems widely adopted in Eurasia for military alert. \n公元前约 1200 年：欧亚大陆广泛使用烽火、狼烟系统进行军事警戒通信。\n≈776 BC Carrier pigeons systematically used in ancient Greece to transmit news. 公元前约 776 年：古希腊规模化使用信鸽传递情报消息。\n≈550 BC Persia establishes the earliest royal horse relay postal network. \n公元前约 550 年：波斯建立最早的皇家驿马接力邮递系统。\n≈200 BC Beacon towers (Fenglui) deployed along China’s northern frontier for military early warning. \n公元前约 200 年：中国北方边境布设烽火台，实现军事预警通信。\n105 AD Cai Lun improves papermaking; lightweight writing medium facilitates message circulation. \n公元 105 年：蔡伦改良造纸术，轻便书写载体推动文书信息流通。\n1792 AD Chappe invents the optical semaphore (visual telegraph), the first standardized long-distance mechanical signalling system. \n公元 1792 年：沙普发明光学信号机（视觉电报），首套标准化远距离机械视觉通信系统。\n1837 Samuel Morse develops Morse Code & the practical electric telegraph. \n1837 年：莫尔斯研制莫尔斯电码与实用有线电报机。\n1844 First official long-distance telegraph message transmitted in the USA. \n1844 年：美国完成第一条正式长途有线电报通信。\n1865 International Telegraph Union (predecessor of ITU) founded in Paris. \n1865 年：国际电报联盟（国际电信联盟 ITU 前身）于巴黎成立。\n1873 Maxwell publishes electromagnetic field theory, predicts electromagnetic waves. 1873 年：麦克斯韦发表电磁场理论，预言电磁波存在。\n1876 Alexander Graham Bell patents the telephone; electrical voice transmission realised. \n1876 年：贝尔取得电话专利，实现语音电信号传输。\n1887 Heinrich Hertz experimentally verifies the existence of electromagnetic waves. \n1887 年：赫兹通过实验证实电磁波真实存在。\n1901 Guglielmo Marconi achieves transatlantic long-distance wireless telegraph transmission.\n 1901 年：马可尼实现跨大西洋远距离无线电报通信。\n1946 AT&T launches the first commercial mobile radiotelephone service (pre-cellular). \n1946 年：美国 AT&T 推出首个商用车载移动无线电话（蜂窝通信雏形）。\n1948 Shannon publishes A Mathematical Theory of Communication, founding modern information theory. \n1948 年：香农发表《通信的数学理论》，奠定现代信息论基础。\n1962 First geosynchronous communication satellite Telstar launched.\n 1962 年：首颗有源同步通信卫星 “电星一号” 发射升空。\n1969 ARPANET established — origin of the modern Internet.\n 1969 年：ARPANET 建成，现代互联网雏形诞生。\n1973 Motorola demonstrates the first handheld cellular mobile phone prototype. \n1973 年：摩托罗拉展示全球首款手持蜂窝移动电话样机。\n1975 Optical fiber communication enters practical engineering application.\n 1975 年：光纤通信技术进入工程实用阶段。\n1981 Nordic Mobile Telephone (NMT) launched: world’s first 1G analog cellular system. \n1981 年：北欧移动电话 NMT 商用，全球第一代模拟蜂窝移动通信（1G）。\n1991 GSM (2G digital cellular communication system) officially deployed in Europe. 1991 年：欧洲正式部署 GSM 第二代数字蜂窝移动通信系统（2G）。\n1995 TCP/IP widely adopted; commercial Internet access expands globally. \n1995 年：TCP/IP 协议大规模普及，商用互联网在全球快速发展。\n2001 WCDMA launched, marking the arrival of 3G mobile broadband communication. \n2001 年：WCDMA 投入商用，标志 3G 移动宽带通信时代到来。\n2009 LTE (4G) standard finalized, enabling high-speed mobile data services. \n2009 年：LTE（4G 通信标准）冻结，支撑高速移动数据业务。\n2019 Large-scale commercial deployment of 5G communication systems begins worldwide. \n2019 年：全球大范围启动 5G 通信系统规模化商用部署。\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nTimeline of Communication Development\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nPart II 通信系统性能指标\n数字通信系统\nThe Beidou-3 navigation system broadcasts open-service and authorized-service navigation signals on B1, B2 and B3 frequency bands.\nB1 band (B1C signal)：Frequency range 1559.052 MHz ~ 1591.788 MHz, bandwidth 32.736 MHz. The navigation message rate is 100 b/s, adopting QMBOC modulation.\nB2 band (B2b signal)：Frequency range 1166.22 MHz ~ 1217.37 MHz, bandwidth 51.15 MHz.\nB3 band (B3I signal)：Frequency range 1250.618 MHz ~ 1286.423 MHz, bandwidth 35.805 MHz.\nThese multi-frequency navigation signals support high-precision positioning, velocity measurement and timing services. The parameters can be used to analyze spectrum allocation and transmission characteristics of satellite navigation links.\n模拟通信系统\nAn AM broadcasting communication system adopts amplitude modulation.
